## Chosen product: **2027 ADHD Planner (printable PDF)**

Why this one:
1. **Timing.** It's October 2026, so buyers are shopping for 2027 planners right now and will keep shopping until January.
2. **Demand vs. difficulty (Ahrefs, US):**
   - `adhd planner`: 6,400 searches/mo, KD 18 (easy)
   - `habit tracker printable`: 6,400/mo, KD 2 (very easy)
   - `adhd planner for adults`: 1,800/mo, KD 4
   - `2027 calendar printable`: 7,700/mo, KD 9
3. **Etsy autocomplete confirms buyer intent.** Etsy suggests `adhd planner printable`, `adhd planner 2027`, `adhd planner adult`, `adhd planner bundle`, `dopamine menu printable`, `dopamine menu adhd`.
4. **Niche angle.** It's more specific than a generic "2027 planner", so it faces less competition and buyers will pay a bit more.
5. **Zero cost to make.** It's built with code: no Canva Pro, no inventory, no shipping.

## Etsy fees (per sale, digital item)
- Listing fee: $0.20 per listing (renews every 4 months or after each sale)
- Transaction fee: 6.5% of sale price
- Payment processing (Pakistan, via Payoneer): 6.5% + $0.30
- Offsite Ads fee: 12-15%, only on sales that come from Etsy's own offsite ads

**Example at $6.99:** 6.99 - 0.20 - 0.45 - 0.75 ≈ **$5.58 profit per sale**

> Etsy Payments is available to sellers in Pakistan through Payoneer (per Etsy's Payments Policy).
