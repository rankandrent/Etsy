# Promotion Plan: 2027 ADHD Planner

Goal: your first 10 sales and first 5 reviews within 30-45 days. Reviews are what make Etsy rank you higher.

## 1. Pinterest (best free traffic for printables)
1. Create a free **Pinterest Business** account with the same name as your shop.
2. Claim your Etsy shop in Pinterest settings (Claimed accounts → Etsy).
3. Make 5 boards: *ADHD Planner Ideas*, *Habit Tracker Printables*, *Dopamine Menu*, *2027 Planner*, *ADHD Tips for Adults*.
4. Pin **3-5 pins a day**: your own image `listing/images/pinterest-pin.jpg` plus the listing images (crop to 2:3), and re-pin other people's helpful content.
5. Every pin links to your Etsy listing URL.

**Pin titles & descriptions (rotate these):**
```
Title: 2027 ADHD Planner Printable for Adults
Desc: A calm, clutter-free ADHD planner with daily time blocks, a habit tracker, dopamine menu and task breakdown pages. Instant PDF download, US Letter + A4. #adhdplanner #habittracker #2027planner #adhd
```

## 2. TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts (free)
Post 1 short video a day for 30 days. No face needed. Film a phone screen or a printed page with music.

Video ideas:
1. "POV: you finally found a planner that doesn't overwhelm your ADHD brain" → flip through pages
2. "My dopamine menu as an ADHD adult" → fill one in
3. "How I beat task paralysis in 3 minutes" → task breakdown page
4. "Plan with me: ADHD daily plan" → time-lapse filling the daily page
5. "3 planner pages every ADHD adult needs for 2027"

Caption: `Link in bio 🌿 #adhd #adhdtiktok #adhdplanner #2027planner #productivity #planwithme`
Bio link: your Etsy shop.

## 3. Reddit / Facebook groups (be careful)
- Only post where self-promotion is allowed (check each group's rules). Most ADHD subs **ban** selling.
- Better: share the **free Dopamine Menu page** as a value post and mention the shop only if people ask.
- Groups: "Etsy Printables", "ADHD Adults Support" (rules first!), r/printables, r/planners (promo threads only).

## 4. Inside Etsy
- **Etsy Ads**: start at **$1-2/day** after your listing has at least 3-5 photos and 1-2 reviews. Check after 14 days and keep only what converts.
- **Sales & coupons**: keep the 30% "Launch Sale" running for the first month.
- **Abandoned-cart coupon**: Marketing → Sales & discounts → auto-send 15% off to people who favourite or abandon the cart.
- **More listings = more traffic.** Add 1 new product a week from `research/keywords.md` → "Next products". Shops with 20+ listings get noticeably more views.
- **Renew** the listing every few weeks (it costs $0.20 and gives a small freshness boost).

| Days | Do this |

| 1 | Open shop, publish listing, set 30% sale, set up Pinterest |
| 2-7 | 3-5 pins/day, 1 short video/day, ask 2-3 friends to buy & honestly review |

| 15 | Turn on Etsy Ads at $1/day |
| 15-30 | Keep pinning + videos; add 1 product/week; reply to every message within 24h |
| 30 | Check Stats: which keywords bring views? Put the best ones first in title/tags |

## Realistic expectations
- Weeks 1-4: views start coming in; first sale usually takes 2-6 weeks for a brand-new shop.
- Months 2-3: with 15-25 listings, consistent pinning and reviews, many printable shops reach 1-5 sales/day.
- At ~$5.58 profit per sale, 3 sales/day ≈ **$500/month**. That's possible but **not guaranteed**: it depends on consistency, reviews, and adding more products.
